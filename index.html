    Meetings booked

    Old report in HubSpot
    CompanyMeetingCredited SDRBooked byBooked onMeeting dateOutcome
    How these meetings are counted
    • Counted by the date the meeting was booked, the day the company moved to Meeting Booked in HubSpot, the same rule as the HubSpot “Meetings Booked” report. The meeting date is shown alongside.
    • First meetings only. Once a company has a deal in the pipeline, follow-up calls belong to the AE and are left out.
    • Credit goes to the company's SDR Owner. Pod leads book many of these meetings, so the old "booked by SDR" report missed them.
    • Some meetings are logged twice. A second HubSpot record titled "[Gong] Google Meet: …" is added by the call-recording integration. The copy gets marked Completed while the original stays Scheduled.
    • Follow-up titles are left out. Meetings titled proposal, pricing, feedback, business case and similar count as AE follow-ups.

    SDR Owner (deal)

    How each number is worked out

    HubSpot properties
    Accounts contacted
    Companies with an SDR Owner whose Last Contacted date (notes_last_contacted) falls in the period. HubSpot sets this automatically whenever a call, sales email, LinkedIn message or meeting is logged against the company or its contacts. It counts activity by anyone, not only the SDR, so an AE's email also counts. It's a "last touch" date, so it can only be read for the current week or month, not rebuilt for a past week. Marketing emails don't count.
    Accounts assigned
    Companies with company SDR Owner (sdr_owner) set, excluding N/A and Annie.
    Coverage
    Accounts contacted ÷ accounts assigned.
    Contacts reached
    Contacts at those companies whose contact Last Contacted date falls in the period.
    Calls
    Call records where Activity assigned to (hubspot_owner_id) is the SDR and the Activity date (hs_timestamp) falls in the period.
    Real conversations
    Calls whose Call outcome (hs_call_disposition) is Connected, Valuable Conversation or Meeting Booked.
    Unique, net-new and follow-up contacts
    From the contact associated to each call. Net-new means the contact's first SDR call of 2026 falls in the period. Follow-up means they had an earlier SDR call this year.
    New vs follow-up accounts
    From SDR calls, each linked to its contact and company. An account or contact is new when this period has its first SDR call of 2026, and follow-up when it was already called earlier in 2026.Emails and LinkedIn aren't included yet. Calls not linked to a company count towards contacts but not accounts.
    Emails sent
    Email records assigned to the SDR with direction "sent". This includes logged calendar invites.
    Sequence emails and enrolments
    Sequence emails: sent emails assigned to the SDR that have a hs_sequence_id. One-off emails are the rest. Contacts enrolled: contacts owned by the SDR whose hs_latest_sequence_enrolled_date falls in the period.Sequence-level enrolments, steps, replies and meetings are lifetime totals from the HubSpot sequences report.
    Email replies
    Email records assigned to the SDR with direction "incoming".Includes out-of-office and other auto-replies, so it's an upper bound.
    Channel mix
    Calls, sequence emails and one-off emails for each SDR. LinkedIn isn't logged in HubSpot yet.
    Quick wins
    This week: first meetings booked (by meeting create date) and held, calls logged as Connected, Valuable Conversation or Bad timing, and sequences created.Always shows this week, whatever the period toggle says.
    Lead response time
    Contacts created since 15 Sep whose Lead Source isn't Sales Outbound, and HubSpot's hs_time_to_first_engagement: time from creation to the current owner's first call, email, meeting outcome or task.
    First meetings booked / held
    Meetings on companies with an SDR Owner, credited to that SDR. Only new first meetings count. Meetings on companies that already had a deal before the meeting was booked, and titles such as proposal, pricing or feedback, are AE follow-ups and are left out.
    Account outcomes
    Company Account Status (account_status), grouped as follows: Handed to AE = Meeting Booked or Open Opportunity. Disqualified = Disqualified or Not Interested. Exhausted = Exhausted. Everything else is still being worked.
    Engagement
    Company Call Outcome – Account engagement (call_outcome__account_engagement) is Connected, Valuable Conversation or Meeting booked.
    Pipeline
    Open deals with deal SDR Owner (sdr_beneficiary_s_) set to one of the SDRs. Value is Amount; Annual recurring revenue (hs_arr) is shown alongside.
    Pods
    Each pod pairs one SDR with a pod lead. Company Pod Catagory (pod_catagory) marks accounts in a pod patch; blank means round-robin.
